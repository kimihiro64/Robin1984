Dedekind RH for K

For a fixed number field K, every zero of ζ_K in 0 < Re s < 1 has Re s = 1/2.

The corrected ideal-divisor inequality

There exists A_K ∈ ℕ such that every nonzero integral ideal 𝔞 of K with N𝔞 ≥ A_K satisfies

σ_K(𝔞) / N𝔞 ≤ eᵞ κ_K [log log N𝔞 + (12 + 3 log |D_K|) / √(log N𝔞)].

N is absolute ideal norm and σ_K(𝔞) sums N𝔟 over every integral ideal divisor 𝔟 of 𝔞. Here D_K is the field discriminant, κ_K is the residue of ζ_K at 1, and γ is Euler's constant.

Dirichlet GRH is equivalent to this inequality in every cyclotomic field ℚ(ζ_m), m ≥ 1, with its own cutoff.

Dedekind ERH is equivalent to this inequality in every number field K, with its own cutoff.
